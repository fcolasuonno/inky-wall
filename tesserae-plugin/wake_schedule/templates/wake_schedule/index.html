{{ card_head('How it works', icon_name='question', level=2) }}
  1. The panel publishes a heartbeat on every wake, before it paints.
  2. For a timer wake, the plugin computes the seconds to the next HH:MM in the list and publishes {"sleep_s": N} to tesserae/<device>/cmd/sleep (retained).
  3. A button wake asks the same question: tap at 17:00 → sleep 1 h to 18:00; tap at 17:30 → sleep 30 min to 18:00.
  4. The firmware applies the directive and clears the topic; a stale one is auto-cleared after 60 s.

Requires firmware inky13-tesserae-0.3 or paperless-L-tesserae. Times use the Tesserae server's local timezone.
